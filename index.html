Designed and implemented all audio from scratch using Wwise and Unity. All SFX and Music are high quality and have been mixed and mastered to comply with industry standards.

Resultare Theme MAIN

  • Ball_Shoot

    A bass-heavy shooting sound that plays when the cannon fires a ball.

  • Shop_ItemBuy

    A synth chime played along coin foley and a long tail that plays when an item is purchased from the shop.

  • Barrier_Break

    A powerful synth one-shot that plays when the "goop" barrier at the bottom of the screen is destroyed.

  • Round_Boss

    A heavy bass synth paired with glass shattering and a police siren that plays when entering a Boss round.

  • Score_Overload

    An electric, wooshy chime that the score counter plays when the player's ball achieves an exceedingly high score.

  • Shop_LegendaryAppear

    A woosh that plays alerting the player that a legendary item appeared in the shop.

  • Ball_Break_Base

    A popping sound that plays when a ball despawns. Different balls have layers that play on top of this sound that are thematic to their type.

  • Menu_Button_Settings_Click

    A short, cute, and satisfying "click" that plays when clicking on a button in the Settings menu.
