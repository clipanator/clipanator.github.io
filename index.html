About

Hi 👋, I'm Matt Warner, a Sound Designer from Ottawa, Ontario

I'm a game audio designer specializing in sound design, interactive music, and technical audio implementation. I started my career learning VFX for film and television, but found my niche when exploring audio. I'm extremely passionate about all things audio and have skills in VFX, motion graphics, 3D modeling, and 3D animation as well. I love diving deep into the technical aspects of different software.
